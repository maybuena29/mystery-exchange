# Privacy Policy & Data Handling — Mystery Exchange

## What Information Is Collected

Mystery Exchange is designed around data minimization:
- **Not Collected**: Real names, display names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or user accounts.
- **Stored**:
  - Group configuration (name, category, dates, budget, instructions, settings).
  - Anonymous song identity (song title and optional artist).
  - Wishlist items (name, description, category, estimated price, optional URLs, priority) and optional preferences.
  - Randomized match assignments (`giver -> recipient`).
  - Private gift-giver checklist state and private notes.

## Visibility Rules

| Data | Public | Organizer | Other Participants | Assigned Gift-Giver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group details & aggregate count | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Completed wishlist count | No | Yes | No | No |
| Song titles & wishlists | No | **No** | **No** | Yes (recipient's only, post-draw) |
| Match assignments | No | **No** | **No** | Own recipient only, post-draw |
| Private gift-giver notes | No | **No** | **No** | Own notes only |

## Known Privacy Limitations

Mystery Exchange provides **practical anonymity**, not absolute anonymity:
1. **Recognizable Songs**: If participants choose a song strongly associated with them in real life, friends may guess who submitted it.
2. **Identifying Wishlist Details**: Including personal links or overly specific notes in a wishlist can reveal identity.
3. **Link Possession**: Anyone who obtains a participant's private link or an organizer's management link can access that role's view. Keep private links confidential.
4. **No Link Recovery**: Because no email or phone number is stored, lost private links cannot be recovered.
